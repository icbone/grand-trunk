2024 was a back-to-the-drawing-board year. GT set out to reshape its image in Toronto open and become the team that draws the city's best players just below GOAT level. Many veterans had moved on, but a solid core returned and a strong group of first-year players joined.

GT finished 3rd at Cinni in Cincinnati against a field of American teams, then 4th at Regionals to qualify for Nationals.

Most of the roster had played one Nationals or none. On day one GT took General Strike to universe point. In a streamed top-8 crossover, GT came back from two breaks down at half to force universe point against Blackfish and earned the turnover, but couldn't convert. GT lost the 9th-place game to Pipe and finished 10th.
