The 2024 roster had 29 players: 20 returning and 9 in their first season with GT. For 16 of them it was their first Nationals with the team, and only six had been more than once. The team built around that young core, with the aim of making GT the destination for Toronto's top players just below GOAT level.

The summer started with a trip to Cinni in Cincinnati, where GT finished 3rd against a field of American teams. At Regionals the team placed 4th and earned its spot at Nationals in Ottawa.

On day one GT took General Strike to universe point and set the tone for the weekend. The top-8 crossover against Blackfish was streamed live. Down 8-5 after the first point of the second half, GT went on a run to tie the game, earned the turn on universe point, and saw Blackfish take it back to win 12-11. GT closed the tournament against Pipe in the 9th-place game and finished 10th.
