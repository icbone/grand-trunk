2024 was a back-to-the-drawing-board year. Coming off 2023, GT set out to reshape its image in Toronto open and become the team that draws the city's best players just below GOAT level. Many veterans had moved on, but a solid core returned, and a strong group of first-year players joined, ready to make an impact as they developed. The team was still competing with Pipe and Hound for up-and-coming talent.

GT finished 3rd at CINI against a field of American teams, then 4th at Regionals to qualify for Nationals. Most of the roster had played one Nationals or none, so the team went in with no expectations and a plan to grind every point.

On day one GT took a strong General Strike team to universe point and put the field on notice. That set up a top-8 crossover against Blackfish, one of the team's two streamed games. Blackfish's deep game, led by one handler's hucks, put GT down a couple of breaks at half. GT answered with a huge run to force universe point and even earned the turnover, but couldn't convert, and Blackfish took it. GT lost the 9th-place game to Pipe and finished 10th, but the season gave the team a strong base to build on.
