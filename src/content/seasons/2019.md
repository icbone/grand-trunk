2019 opened a new chapter. Four first-time captains led a 23-player roster with 13 players in their first GT season, and [Daniel Wong](/grand-trunk/players/daniel-wong), in his fourth season, as the most experienced player on the team.

The summer was defined by a rivalry with Hound, a team started in 2018 by former GT players. The two teams traded tight games all season.

They met again at Nationals in Edmonton, in a crossover for a spot in the top 8. In heavy wind, a single break decided it for Hound. GT finished the weekend by beating Manic in the 9th-place game to place 9th.
