2019 was a full reset. Jaleel Paje and many veterans moved on, and four first-time captains led a young roster that was hungry and willing to grind.

The season's big rivalry was with Hound, a team started in 2018 by former GT players. The two teams played a run of tight games all summer.

The last one came at Nationals in Edmonton: a crossover for a spot in the top 8, played in miserable, windy conditions, where a single break decided it in Hound's favour. GT beat Manic in the 9th-place game to finish 9th.
