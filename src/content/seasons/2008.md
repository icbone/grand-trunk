Grand Trunk brought a veteran handler core and a deep defensive line into the 2008 Nationals, but a hard-cap loss to Sofa Kings and a heavy one to Invictus left the team needing results on Friday.

Friday turned the tournament. After a disputed point at 3-5, GT's 1-3-2-1 zone shut Firebird out and the team scored 12 straight points to win 15-5. Against Blackfish, a call neither side would give up was settled by rock-paper-scissors, and GT came back to win 12-11 on universe point after losing all five of its universe points that season.

That was enough for the top 8. GT lost its quarterfinal 11-15 to the top-seeded Nomads, beat General Strike 12-11 on another universe point, then lost the game for 5th to Phoenix to finish 6th.
