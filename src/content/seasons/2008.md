Grand Trunk opened the 2008 Nationals with a win over Demon. Then a hard-cap loss to Sofa Kings and a heavy one to Invictus left the team needing results on Friday.

It got them. Against Firebird, a disputed point at 3-5 fired the team up. GT's 1-3-2-1 zone shut Firebird out from there, and GT scored 12 straight points to win 15-5. Next came Blackfish, who had just upset Mephisto. With soft cap on and a call that neither side would give up, the two captains settled the dispute with rock-paper-scissors. GT lost the throw and fell behind, then came back to win 12-11 on universe point. The team had lost all five of its universe points that season before this one.

That was enough for the top 8. GT lost its quarterfinal 11-15 to the top-seeded Nomads, then beat General Strike 12-11 on another universe point. That win also made up for a 15-5 loss to General Strike at the 2007 Nationals. GT lost the game for 5th to Phoenix and finished 6th.
