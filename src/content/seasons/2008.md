The 2008 team went to Nationals with a handler core of Mike Yeomans, Ryan Keyfitz and Justin McComb, and a defensive line built around its 1-3-2-1 zone.

Thursday opened with a 15-7 win over Demon, followed by games against Sofa Kings and Invictus. On Friday the zone took over against Firebird: after a disputed point at 3-5, GT scored 12 straight to win 15-5. Against Blackfish, an unresolved call was settled by rock-paper-scissors, and GT won 12-11 on universe point, the team's first universe-point win of the season.

That put GT in the top 8. After a quarterfinal against the top-seeded Nomads, GT beat General Strike 12-11 on another universe point, with Ryan Keyfitz catching the winner in the endzone. GT played Phoenix for 5th and finished 6th.
