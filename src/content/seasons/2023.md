The 2023 team built on 2022: 17 of its 30 players returned, 16 of them from the year before. Several veterans also played for the Toronto Rush or practised with GOAT, which kept them busy through much of the summer.

GT finished 4th at Montreal Jazzfest and 4th at No Borders.

At Regionals, GT met Too Bad, one of Toronto's longest-running open teams, in a game that went to universe point. Too Bad took it, and GT finished the weekend by winning the 6th-place game to place 6th.
