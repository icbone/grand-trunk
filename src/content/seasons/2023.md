After the breakthrough of 2022, GT added more experienced players to its young core and went into 2023 with higher expectations. The summer brought two strong results, 4th at Montreal Jazzfest and 4th at No Borders.

Building chemistry was the challenge. Several of the team's key veterans also played for the Toronto Rush or were on GOAT's practice roster, so they missed most GT practices, and the full team had few reps together before Regionals. There, GT lost on universe point to Too Bad, one of Toronto's longest-running open teams, which cost the team its shot at the game for a Nationals spot. GT won the 6th-place game to finish 6th.

It was a year of change in Toronto open, with a new team, Pipe, forming alongside a consistently strong Hound.
