After the breakthrough of 2022, GT added more experienced players to its young core and went into 2023 with higher expectations. Several key veterans also played for the Toronto Rush or were on GOAT's practice roster, so the full team had few reps together.

The summer still brought two strong results: 4th at Montreal Jazzfest and 4th at No Borders.

At Regionals, GT lost on universe point to Too Bad, one of Toronto's longest-running open teams, which cost it a shot at the game for a Nationals spot. GT won the 6th-place game to finish 6th.
