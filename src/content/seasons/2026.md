2026 was another reset, a familiar pattern for the B team in Toronto's club system. Only about six players returned, most of them rookies the year before, so the team started with no set expectations and a lot of young talent keen to learn.

The road was rocky. A 9th-place finish at Montreal Jazzfest was one of GT's lowest there in years, and the young team had its highs and lows, including losses to teams it hadn't lost to in a long time.

It came through when it mattered: 2nd at Regionals, one of GT's best Regionals finishes in years, and 8th at Nationals, the team's first top-8 finish since the 2018 bronze.
