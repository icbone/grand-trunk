2018 was [Jaleel Paje](/grand-trunk/players/jaleel-paje)'s last season running Grand Trunk. To send him off with a medal, a number of GT alumni who had moved up to GOAT came back to play that summer.

The team built toward Nationals with one goal: a podium finish for the coach who had run the team since 2014.

At Nationals, GT met Alberta Flatball Club in the semifinal and couldn't stop their deep game. The loss sent GT to the bronze-medal game, which they won comfortably to finish 3rd.
