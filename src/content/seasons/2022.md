2022 was a hard reset. The roster was almost entirely rookies, joined by a few veterans returning after time off. Many of the rookies had lost their junior years to COVID-19 and were jumping straight into senior ultimate, so the team started the season with no real expectations. What it did have was a great group dynamic, plus coaching from [Danyal Amjad](/grand-trunk/players/danyal-amjad), back with the team, and [Nicolas Edwards](/grand-trunk/players/nicolas-edwards).

Toronto open was crowded that year. Many GT alumni had joined Hound to build a super team, and Boat, a long-running team that had never been at GT's level, brought in other former GT players for a "final voyage" season. That left GT as the third of the three teams.

The highlight came at Regionals. Not sure it would even qualify, GT upset Hound on universe point, throwing the national seeding into chaos and rewarding a summer of work by a group that believed in each other. At Nationals the young team finished 9th, just outside the top 8, but simply making Nationals with that roster was a big result.
