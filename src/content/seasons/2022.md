Sixteen of the 26 players on the 2022 roster were in their first GT season, many of them jumping into senior ultimate after losing their junior years to COVID-19. [Danyal Amjad](/grand-trunk/players/danyal-amjad) returned to coach, with support from [Nicolas Edwards](/grand-trunk/players/nicolas-edwards).

Toronto open had three strong teams that year. Many GT alumni had joined Hound, and Boat brought in other former GT players for a "final voyage" season.

At Regionals, GT beat Hound on universe point, shaking up the national seeding and finishing 2nd. The team went on to Nationals in Brampton and finished 9th, a summer of growth for a young group.
