2022 was a hard reset. The roster was almost entirely rookies, many of whom had lost their junior years to COVID-19, joined by a few veterans returning after time off. [Danyal Amjad](/grand-trunk/players/danyal-amjad) came back to coach, with support from [Nicolas Edwards](/grand-trunk/players/nicolas-edwards).

Toronto open was crowded. Many GT alumni had joined Hound to build a super team, and Boat brought in other former GT players for a "final voyage" season, leaving GT as the third of the three teams.

The highlight came at Regionals, where GT upset Hound on universe point and finished 2nd. At Nationals the young team finished 9th, just outside the top 8, but simply making Nationals with that roster was a big result.
