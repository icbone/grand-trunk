2021 was the first season back after COVID-19 cancelled 2020. The 30-player roster split evenly: 15 returning players, most from the 2019 team, and 15 in their first GT season, several of whom would form the team's core for years.

The season was short. Tryouts were smaller, play started in August instead of May, and GT played one tournament before the national event. With no time for regionals or qualifiers, Ultimate Canada replaced CUC with the Ultimate Canada Invitational (UCI), open to the teams that had qualified in 2019.

At UCI, GT closed its weekend with a win over Regiment of Newfoundland in the 11th-place game to finish 11th.
