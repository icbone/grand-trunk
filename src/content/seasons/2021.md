2021 was the first season back after COVID-19 cancelled 2020. On paper GT was strong, building on the 2019 group and adding several young players who would become a core part of the team for years.

The season was short. Tryouts were smaller, play started in August instead of May, and there was only one tournament before the national event. With no time for regionals or qualifiers, CUC was replaced by the Ultimate Canada Invitational (UCI), open to the teams that had qualified in 2019.

The team never quite came together at UCI. GT beat Regiment of Newfoundland in the 11th-place game to finish 11th, one of its lowest finishes at that point.
