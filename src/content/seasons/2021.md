2021 was the first season back after COVID-19 cancelled 2020. It was a short one. Tryouts were smaller, and the season started in August instead of the usual May, with only one tournament before the national event.

There was no time for regionals or qualifiers, so CUC was replaced by the Ultimate Canada Invitational (UCI), run like Nationals and open to the teams that had qualified in 2019. On paper GT was strong, building on the 2019 group and adding several young players who would become a core part of the team for years. It never quite came together, though. GT finished 11th, one of the lowest finishes in team history at that point, after playing Regiment of Newfoundland in the 11th-place game.
