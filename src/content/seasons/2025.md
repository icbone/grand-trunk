A strong core returned from 2024, joined by new captains, a new coach in [Mauricio Martinez Lung](/grand-trunk/players/mauricio-martinez-lung), and strong rookies to push the roster.

A 3rd-place finish at Montreal Jazzfest raised expectations. At Regionals, GT lost a tight game to Hound on universe point that would have put the team in the top two, then beat Moby in the game-to-go to secure a spot at Nationals.

With the top 8 as the goal, GT came up just short at Nationals and finished 10th after losing the 9th-place game to Quake.
