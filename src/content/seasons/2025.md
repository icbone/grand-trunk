The 2025 roster brought back 16 of its 28 players, with 12 in their first GT season. New captains led the team, and [Mauricio Martinez Lung](/grand-trunk/players/mauricio-martinez-lung) joined as coach.

GT opened the summer with 3rd place at Montreal Jazzfest. At Regionals, a universe-point game against Hound decided the top two, and GT then beat Moby in the game-to-go to earn its place at Nationals.

At Nationals, GT played Quake in the 9th-place game and finished 10th.
