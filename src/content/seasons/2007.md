By 2007, Grand Trunk had been through heavy turnover in its four years, the cost of being the middle team in a club system where the best players move up to GOAT. Ten of the 26 players had been on the 2006 team. The squad was the deepest since the first season, but it took a while to come together.

GT opened 6-1 at No Surf, then struggled through a run of losses at the Boston Invitational. No Borders in Ottawa was the best tournament of the year: GT beat Firebird 15-5 and lost in the quarters to the eventual winners.

At Nationals in Toronto, GT finished 10th, beating teams on the last day that had beaten them earlier in the year.
