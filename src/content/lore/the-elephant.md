Grand Trunk has played under an elephant since its first season.

## The original elephant

In the 2004 team photo, the players are wearing grey shirts printed with the original logo: a cartoon elephant, ears out and tusks forward, set inside a dark circle. *Image to come.*

## The current logo

The current logo turns the elephant to face straight on, with grey ears and white tusks against an orange circle. The team's navy and orange come from it, and so do the colours of this site.
