[Peter Jamieson](/grand-trunk/players/peter-jamieson) played for Grand Trunk from its first season and co-captained the team in 2006. From 2006 to 2010 he kept a blog, *The Cultimate Opinion*, about ultimate in Toronto and beyond. Here is what it adds to the team's story.

## From Pong to Grand Trunk

Peter was one of the handlers who came over from Pong when Grand Trunk started. He later wrote that the move was humbling: a team full of players who had each been the main handler on their old teams, none of them sure yet how to share the disc.

He described Grand Trunk as the team that "filled the gap between elite Ultimate and elite development." It was built around key players who came through the University of Toronto. In its first year the team's talent caught GOAT's eye, half the roster moved up, and that crossover led to the three-team open system Toronto still uses.

## The first season, 2004

Peter's tournament records show how strong the inaugural year was:

- **Won the open division at the Boston Invitational.** Years later he still called it one of the best-run, most competitive tournaments on the calendar.
- Tied for 3rd–4th at Montreal Jazzfest and 4th at Motown Throwdown in Detroit.
- Played Canadian Nationals in Vancouver, then the UPA series: 3rd at Sectionals in Rochester and 8th at Regionals in Boston.

## 2006: a huge leap

Grand Trunk went into the 2006 Nationals as one of several teams chasing GOAT. Mephisto beat them 15-5 on the first day, but GT beat Phoenix in a crossover and finished **3rd**. Peter called it a huge leap for the team.

That season [Mike Yeomans](/grand-trunk/players/mike-yeomans) also taught the team a cutting drill that Peter later named "Yeomans Dog" and kept running with other teams.

## 2007: rebuilding

By 2007, Peter noted, Grand Trunk had been through heavy turnover in its four years. That was the cost of being the middle team in a club system where the best players move up to GOAT. The 2007 squad was the deepest since the first season, but it took a while to come together:

- **No Surf:** 6 wins and 1 loss. Peter said the team learned the most from the one loss.
- **Boston Invitational:** a run of losses, including one to Red Circus.
- **No Borders:** the best tournament of the year. GT beat Firebird 15-5 and lost in the quarters to the eventual winners.
- **Motown Throwdown:** GT made itself the Toronto crowd for the GOAT vs. BAT final, cheering GOAT on.
- **Nationals in Toronto:** GT finished 10th, beating teams on the last day that had beaten them earlier in the year.

Four Grand Trunk alumni were also playing for Vancouver's Blackfish that year, which made them something of a western offshoot of GT.

## 2008

Peter moved to London in 2008, the year he expected to captain the team. He followed GT from abroad and called the 2008 Nationals the team's second-best ever.
