## From Pong to Grand Trunk

Several of Grand Trunk's first handlers came over from Pong. They had each been the main handler on their old teams, and at first the new team had to work out how a group of alpha handlers would share the disc.

Grand Trunk was the bridge in Toronto between elite ultimate and the development level below it. Many of its key players came through the University of Toronto. In its first year the team's talent caught GOAT's eye and half the roster moved up. That crossover led to the three-team open system Toronto still uses.

## The first season, 2004

The first season was a strong one:

- **Won the open division at the Boston Invitational**, one of the most competitive and best-run tournaments on the calendar.
- Tied for 3rd–4th at Montreal Jazzfest and 4th at Motown Throwdown in Detroit.
- 8th at Canadian Nationals in Vancouver, then the UPA series: 3rd at Sectionals in Rochester and 8th at Regionals in Boston.

## 2006: a huge leap

Grand Trunk went into the 2006 Nationals as one of several teams chasing GOAT. Mephisto beat them 15-5 on the first day, but GT beat Phoenix in a crossover and finished **3rd**, a big step up for the team.

That season [Mike Yeomans](/grand-trunk/players/mike-yeomans) taught the team a cutting drill that became known as "Yeomans Dog".

## 2007: rebuilding

By 2007, Grand Trunk had been through heavy turnover in its four years. That was the cost of being the middle team in a club system where the best players move up to GOAT. The 2007 squad was the deepest since the first season, but it took a while to come together:

- **No Surf:** 6 wins and 1 loss, with the team learning the most from the one loss.
- **Boston Invitational:** a run of losses, including one to Red Circus.
- **No Borders:** the best tournament of the year. GT beat Firebird 15-5 and lost in the quarters to the eventual winners.
- **Motown Throwdown:** GT made itself the Toronto crowd for the GOAT vs. BAT final, cheering GOAT on.
- **Nationals in Toronto:** GT finished 10th, beating teams on the last day that had beaten them earlier in the year.

Four Grand Trunk alumni were also playing for Vancouver's Blackfish that year, which made them something of a western offshoot of GT.

## 2008

The 2008 Nationals were GT's second-best yet. The team won two universe points on the way to 6th place. [The 2008 season page](/grand-trunk/seasons/2008) has the full story.
