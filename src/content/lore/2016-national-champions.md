In August 2016, at the Canadian Ultimate Championships in Edmonton, Grand Trunk won its first national title. Going in, the team had never finished higher than third at CUC, and that bronze was a decade old.

## The road to the final

Grand Trunk came in as the #3 seed. Many of the country's biggest clubs skipped Nationals that year to focus on the Triple Crown Tour, and Trunk made the most of the opening. Along the way they beat top seed Mio Grape, a new Vancouver team, on universe point in the wind.

## The final: Grand Trunk 15, General Strike 12

Winnipeg's General Strike was also chasing its first Canadian title. The teams traded holds to 5-5, with Jeffrey Woo hauling in three of Toronto's early goals on deep shots. Trunk's defence punished a couple of late Winnipeg mistakes for an 8-6 halftime lead.

Winnipeg got its only break of the game after half to pull within one. Trunk answered with a quick hold and a break of its own to go up 11-8, and never gave the lead back. At 12-10, Cooper Toogood chased down a deflected huck and made a one-handed layout catch on the sideline to score. Connor Armstrong finished it with another layout grab for the winning point.

Armstrong was named MVP of the final, fresh off a silver medal at the World Junior Ultimate Championships. His connection with Paul Tatulea, who had four assists and a goal, ran Trunk's offence all weekend.

## A young team

Much of the roster hadn't toured before. Captains Derek Fan and Danyal Amjad credited the work the team put into practices and tournaments. Amjad summed up the program's identity: "This is a team that always picks up young, talented, athletes."
