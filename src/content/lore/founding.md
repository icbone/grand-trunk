## Toronto open before 2004

By 2001, Toronto's open scene was spread across a handful of teams: Redi Knights, Y.E.S., Nerve, Love Gravy, Too Bad and Dead Baby Seals, with Wax playing out of Ottawa.

In 2002 things consolidated. Redi Knights, Y.E.S., Nerve and Ottawa's Wax combined into **GOAT**, a joint Toronto–Ottawa team. Love Gravy carried on as **Joystik**, and a new team, **Pong**, appeared. Too Bad kept going as it always had.

## Before Grand Trunk: Joystik

GOAT was Toronto's top open team, but its roster was split evenly across Toronto, Ottawa and Montreal, so only six or seven players were local for practices. Joystik was the old guard of Toronto's top players, legends like Simo, Robin Coster, Derek Baxter, Daryl Chow, Jim Lim and Joe Aziz. It practiced once a week and never trained with GOAT.

Joystik finished 3rd at Nationals in 2003, and some of its younger players saw a chance to do even better. After the season, [Inian](/grand-trunk/players/inian-moorthy) and a teammate asked Joystik's leadership to move to two practices a week and make a run at a Canadian title. After a lot of deliberation, Joystik's players, busy with families and jobs, kept their commitment the same.

## The breakaway

So in 2004 the younger players decided it was time for a truly Toronto-based team that could compete with GOAT. They came from a few places:

- the younger players on **Joystik**, including [Phil](/grand-trunk/players/phil-watanabe), [Shooter](/grand-trunk/players/scott-hastie), [Dean](/grand-trunk/players/dean-ruhnke), [Inian](/grand-trunk/players/inian-moorthy), [Magnum](/grand-trunk/players/david-pickwoad) and [Kirk](/grand-trunk/players/kirk-nylen);
- the **University of Toronto**, including [Peter](/grand-trunk/players/peter-jamieson), [Chua](/grand-trunk/players/shawn-chua) and [Peyton](/grand-trunk/players/peyton-leung);
- one or two younger players from **Black Sheep**, a mixed team that later became Bombing Madd Fatties and then Union;
- some of the best up-and-coming young players from **Pong**.

Joystik and U of T were the biggest influences on the first team, and GT picked up others along the way. **[Colin Hanes](/grand-trunk/players/colin-hanes)**, who came over from Black Sheep and the Fatties, came up with the name.

The same year, Pong disbanded after two seasons (2002–2003). Its players split two ways: a core of them formed **Roy**, and others went to Grand Trunk. Toronto's club history traces both Roy and Grand Trunk back to Pong.

## The first season

Grand Trunk practiced Tuesdays and Thursdays. On Thursdays GOAT practiced at the same field at Riverdale Park, and the two teams often scrimmaged together at the end of the night: GT's 25 players against GOAT's six or so locals. GT often won. Taking over GOAT's field caused some friction between the teams.

The inaugural season was a strong one. Grand Trunk beat Joystik and became the top all-Toronto team. Along the way they picked up the odd extra player for tournaments like No Surf in Cleveland and Motown Throwdown in Detroit.

## Birth of the club system

Grand Trunk caught GOAT's attention. After the 2004 season, Hass, Dime, [Inian](/grand-trunk/players/inian-moorthy) and another GT leader brokered a merger, and 14 players from the 2004 Grand Trunk team joined GOAT for 2005. GOAT became an almost entirely Toronto team, and 2005 was a rough year for Grand Trunk as it looked for a new identity.

The merger created a formal farm system in Toronto that lasted more than a decade. GOAT, Grand Trunk and Roy held tryouts together, and their leaders coordinated roster selection. Tryout players for GOAT and GT practiced and scrimmaged together in "May GOAT League", with final teams picked after the TUF tournament. GOAT regularly called up players from GT, and GT did the same with Roy. Too Bad offered a home for players who didn't fit the club system.

Joystik played one more season, in 2005, then folded. Its players later regrouped with former GOAT players as **Tombstone**, competing in the masters and grand masters divisions. The irony: once the under-30s left to form Grand Trunk, everyone still on Joystik was already masters-eligible.
